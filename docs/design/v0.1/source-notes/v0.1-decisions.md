# v0.1 Design Decisions

Public rationale for decisions referenced by code comments and regression tests.

## Why move version into the brand chip?

The version belongs near the product identity so operators can report it during
support without scanning settings pages. The footer remains reserved for the
account card.

## Why use an account popover?

v0.1 is single-admin. Account actions are low-frequency and fit naturally in the
sidebar footer. A full Account settings page would imply broader profile or team
configuration that v0.1 does not ship.

## Why is Change password a modal?

Password change is a rare account action in v0.1. A small modal keeps it close to
the account popover while avoiding a permanent settings page for a one-off task.

## Why keep Settings system-focused?

Settings controls host and product integrations. Personal-account actions stay in
the account popover until multi-user account management exists.
